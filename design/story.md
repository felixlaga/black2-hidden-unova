# The Roads We Choose — N and Alder

**Design only. The base ROM is supplied, but no modified ROM, executable expansion story, or tested new encounter exists yet.** Canonical authoring data: [story.json](../content/story.json). Battle proposals: [difficulty.json](../config/difficulty.json).

N replaces Iris as Champion at the end of **one larger main campaign: eight original Gyms plus twelve additional Gyms**. The expanded regions are part of that adventure. Every area, all 649 Pokémon, unique items, upgrades and facilities must be available before N's final battle. Earlier Gyms, exploration, clues and main-story tasks may gate progress; becoming Champion may not.

The user authorized only the original dialogue changes necessary for this story and its revised access rules. Every unrelated original line stays unchanged. Exact text exceptions, map bindings and event IDs require the original ROM; none have been invented here.

## Seven main-story chapters

1. **A Place to Stop.** After the first original Badge, Alder and his Volcarona help a refuge keeper. N meets the player while observing Pokémon choose where to rest. Alder begins a recurring coaching role.
2. **Two Different Paces.** N and the rival disagree about rushing through a crowded habitat. The player finds a quieter route. N and Alder offer practice battles; losing them never blocks exploration.
3. **Room for Everyone.** The early expansion Gyms and towns are already open during the main adventure. N returns at several milestones. Alder's traveling school adapts to each settlement instead of imposing the same lesson everywhere.
4. **Taking Our Turns.** During the middle and later Gyms, a washed-out route separates refuges. The player, rival and N share the repair work. Alder learns to let his students lead. The restored watershed supplies Keldeo's clue trail.
5. **Keeping the Light.** After the original crisis and eighth original Badge, Alder accepts help keeping a shelter open. N's dragon encounter and related stone/story events move into this pre-finale chapter. Capture remains optional and permanently available.
6. **A Champion's Open Door.** Before the League, Alder offers his strongest optional Volcarona battle. All twelve additional Gyms and the exploration network are main-campaign content. Darkrai's quest is available now; N does not have to be defeated first.
7. **The Promise at the League.** N is the final Champion opponent. His battle pays off repeated meetings through the adventure. Victory ends the main story without unlocking any exclusive area, species, unique item or upgrade.

N's signature partner is Zoroark. His final team uses Darmanitan rather than a Zekrom already transferred to the player. The original dragon ownership and encounter chronology need a full in-ROM audit; there must be one coherent, permanently available pre-finale acquisition path.

Alder is the orange-haired former Champion the user referred to. He remains an active mentor, accompanies regional tasks, appears in multiple battles, and offers his strongest battle **before** the final League. Post-ending rematches are optional repeats.

## Three permanent secret quests

- **Victini — A Small Victory.** Victory Ember Hearth in Copperbrook, sharing the existing Bellows Den slot. After five original Badges and three new Gyms, follow a training scoreboard, an oven log and a split-wing tile. Repair the hearth and report; Victini is proposed at level 45. The ordinary Growlithe resident remains.
- **Keldeo — The Patient Crossing.** Oathwater Crossing in Dawnbridge, sharing the River Crown slot. Before N, with eight original Badges and ten new Gyms, follow a ferryman's sketch, reed marks and spillway stones. Restore a safe crossing; Keldeo is proposed at level 65. The ordinary Kingdra resident remains.
- **Darkrai — A Quiet Light.** Hushlantern Sanctuary in Dawnbridge, sharing the Night Orchard slot. Before N, with eight original Badges and twelve new Gyms, follow a guestbook, a shutter pattern and crescent trail marks. Restore a quiet refuge; Darkrai is proposed at level 70. The ordinary Umbreon resident remains.

These are three of the existing **96** secret places, not three additional map slots. The full clue chains and original NPC dialogue are in the JSON. Sites remain accessible; the special encounter waits for its ordinary main-campaign prerequisites. All three quests are offline and permanent, with no expired event, ticket distribution, trading, real-night window or other unique Pokémon required.

Declining, fleeing, defeating the encounter or losing the battle must allow a retry. Only a successful, consistently saved capture resolves the one-time encounter. Quest completion never requires capture and never removes an uncaught visitor. Physical exits remain usable in every state. These are requirements awaiting implementation and save/reload tests.

## Postgame

Search for the former Team Plasma sages in **already accessible places**, revisit the world, and take rematches. Any ordinary supply rewards are already available earlier. No new area, exclusive Pokémon, unique item, upgrade or facility is reserved for postgame.

This rule also applies to **original Black 2 content**, not just the additions. Base-game barriers, encounters, dragon events, evolution methods, version exclusives and facility unlocks need an access audit and migration before the final battle. The design does not yet prove all-649 availability.
