# Hidden Unova — playing this build

Start a new game in `build/black2-hidden-unova.nds`. Keep its save separate from the original Black 2. The supplied QA saves are only for development tests.

## Finding the new regions

Talk to the new trail guides on the original routes. Their paths open as your original badge count grows:

| Entrance | Opens after | Region |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Route 20 | Cheren, first original badge | Southworks Greenbelt |
| Route 6 | Clay, fifth original badge | Western Basin |
| Route 7 | Skyla, sixth original badge | Highmarch |
| Route 21 | Marlon, eighth original badge | Eastern Reach |
| Castelia Sewers | Third original badge and the Castelia inquiry | Underground route to Copperbrook |

Begin the Castelia inquiry with the Battle Company receptionist. Follow the six building clues before looking for the sewer guide. New towns have a rest house and a field office; talk to their residents for local investigations and story leads.

Resolve the regional Team Plasma incidents as you travel. N and Alder appear in the new towns. The final Dawnbridge incident also requires defeating Ghetsis in the original story. Its resolution opens the migrated original late-game routes and facilities before the final League battle.

## Secret Pokémon

All 649 Generation 1–5 species have assigned native encounter, gift/static, or evolution sources. Trading evolutions have offline alternatives. The full acquisition route has not yet been completed in a playthrough.

Victini, Keldeo and Darkrai each have three ordered clues, three repairs you can do in any order, and a local keeper to report to. Repairs unlock after all three clues. Read the clues for the next location. These quests are permanent and available during the main campaign; they need no online event or date change.

An uncaught static encounter can be tried again after fleeing or defeating it. Once that species is registered as caught anywhere in your Pokédex, its static offer stops. Some species appear through a naturalist's dialogue because a matching native walking sprite is unavailable.

## After N

The optional sages search visits field offices in Bramblewick, Reedwake, Sandglass Court, Threadmill Crossing, Frostmere Hearth and Dawnbridge. Return to Rood after speaking with all six. This provides story closure without unlocking an exclusive area, species or item.

## Current testing limits

This is a development candidate with implemented maps, battles, quests and access changes. Focused binary checks and representative emulator interactions pass. A complete new-save campaign and all-649 capture run remain unverified. The 3DS XL/flashcart has not been tested; see [HARDWARE.md](HARDWARE.md).
